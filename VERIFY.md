# Data sources and known gaps

All character-creation data was checked against *RuneQuest: Roleplaying in Glorantha* (2nd printing):
- homelands and regional modifiers (pp. 24–27, 45, 53, 60–63, Homelands chapter)
- runes and characteristics (pp. 44–59)
- occupations (pp. 63–73)
- cults (pp. 73–80 and the Rune Cults chapter)
- personal skills and other information (pp. 79–83)
- spirit magic (pp. 253–268), Rune magic (pp. 313–350), sorcery (pp. 381–402)
- skills, weapons, armor and equipment (Skills, Combat and Equipment chapters)

Data entries are marked `source: 'book'`. The engine is tested against the book's worked example, Vasana (pp. 45–59).

## Known gaps
- **Family history** (Step 2, pp. 27–44): the year-by-year event tables are not reproduced. Players roll them from the book and record the results (passions, skill bonuses, Reputation). The book's skip option is explained in the app.
- **Whip** skill: used by Chariot Drivers but missing from the core weapon tables; its base is set at 5%.
- **Fisher's Thrown Net** option is not included (no base chance in the weapon tables).
- **Weapon picks** for "1H weapon" accept any melee skill. Light Cavalry's six unit-weapon sets aren't enforced exactly.
- **Size limits** on summon and dismiss elemental spells, and Seven Mothers' elemental limits, appear in cult hints only.
- **Assistant Shaman:** the free Second Sight and the +1 spirit combat damage are noted, not applied.
- **Philosopher plus Lhankor Mhy sorcery:** how the two combine isn't in the book. The app merges their masteries.
- **Regional passions** apply when you first pick a region. If you switch regions, adjust passions by hand.
- **Logical Clarity** (sorcery): the spell's icon and the worked example on p. 386 disagree about its Rune. The data uses the icon.
