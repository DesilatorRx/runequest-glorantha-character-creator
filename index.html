RuneQuest Character Creator (Roleplaying in Glorantha)

RuneQuest Initiate is a free, open-source RuneQuest character creator for RuneQuest: Roleplaying in Glorantha. Build an adventurer in your browser: homeland, rune affinities, characteristics, family passions, occupation, cult initiation, skills, spirit and rune magic, and equipment, with live tallies and rule checks at every step.

No install and no account. Adventurers are saved in your browser.
